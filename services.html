The 4 Stages of Success

We don't just advise you—we build the strategy, create the assets, and run the campaigns to get you funded.

01 SECURE

The Foundation of Your Campaign. We handle the heavy lifting of finding the money. Whether it's crowdfunding or grants, we position your project as a prime candidate for funding.

  • Crowdfunding Strategy: We build your pre-launch funnel, set up your campaign page, and manage your live campaign on Kickstarter, Indiegogo, Seed&Spark, and GoFundMe.
  • Grant Writing: We research and identify the perfect grants for your project (NEA, Sundance, etc.) and write compelling, winning proposals.

Base Camp

02 LAUNCH

Make Some Noise. You have the funding strategy; now we make sure the world knows about it. We create the visual and media assets that turn viewers into backers.

  • Pitch Deck Design: We design investor-grade pitch decks that clearly communicate your vision and secure meetings.
  • Press Release & PR: We write and distribute media-ready announcements to get your project featured in relevant publications.
  • Campaign Video Strategy: We script and direct the core video assets that drive your campaign's conversion.

The Ascent

03 GROW

Scale Your Momentum. Launch day is just the beginning. We use paid ads and organic reach to build a massive wave of backers and keep the momentum going.

  • Ads Management: We run highly targeted Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and Google ads to drive high-quality traffic to your campaign.
  • Social Media Marketing: We build your community, manage your social channels, and create engaging content to keep backers excited.
  • SEO Optimisation: We make sure your project gets found organically by people searching for what you're creating.

Summit Push

04 SUSTAIN

Keep the Momentum Alive. Once the campaign ends, the real work begins. We help you convert your backers into long-term fans and customers.

  • Investor Relations: We help you manage post-campaign communication with your backers and investors.
  • Community Management: We nurture your audience so they are ready to support your next launch.
  • Advisory: We provide long-term brand strategy and quarterly growth audits to ensure your business keeps growing.

The Peak
